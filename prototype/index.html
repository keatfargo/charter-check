Did the meeting match the charter?

Upload what your team promised on paper and what actually happened in the room. You get per-person scores, transcript quotes, and honest gaps—not a grade for the professor.

What Charter Check does

It lines up your team charter (roles and responsibilities) with a meeting transcript and shows whether each duty showed up in the conversation—with quotes as proof. Use it for retros and “are we doing what we agreed?” check-ins.

How to use it

  1. 1 Click Start a review, then Try sample team for a ready-made charter and transcript—no uploads.
  2. 2 Or choose Upload your files, add your charter and transcript (.txt, .md, .pdf, or .docx), and run live AI analysis.
  3. 3 Open the report tabs: Overview (big picture), By member (score and quotes for every person and duty), then Gaps (what to improve next time).
